Privacy Policy

How your documents are processed, where results are stored, and the choices you control.

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Your documents are processed on your device.

ContextLite converts selected PDFs into Markdown locally. It does not upload your documents to the developer or an AI service. Saving, copying, and sharing happen when you choose those actions.

1. About this policy

This policy covers the ContextLite Android application (package name dev.contextlite), provided by Adi Rahman, and this privacy policy website.

ContextLite prepares text-based PDFs for use in other workflows by converting their contents into Markdown. It does not require an account and does not connect to an AI model or use your documents to train one.

2. Documents and information the app accesses

When you select PDFs through Android’s document picker, ContextLite reads the selected documents and their filenames. It processes document text, page counts, file sizes, and document references to produce Markdown, identify sources, estimate token counts, and show conversion progress or errors.

Document contents may include personal information that you choose to process. This information is handled locally for the conversion you request. ContextLite does not scan your entire device or request broad access to all files.

The app may retain Android’s read permission for selected documents so a conversion can continue in the background or resume after an interruption. Permissions for documents no longer needed are released as selections and conversion batches change.

3. Local storage, retention, and deletion

  • Latest conversion: the app stores its most recent conversion state in private app storage, including document references and filenames, extracted Markdown, progress, metrics, and errors. This allows results to remain available after reopening the app. Starting a new conversion replaces the previous saved conversion state.
  • Recovery and preferences: local work records and notification preferences support background processing. An unreadable conversion checkpoint may be retained locally for recovery diagnosis until app data is cleared.
  • Temporary files: PDF processing may use the app’s cache. Sharing creates a cached Markdown copy. Share copies older than seven days are removed the next time you use Share; this is not a scheduled seven-day deletion. Android may also remove cached files.

To remove ContextLite’s internal data and retained document permissions, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Clearing only the cache does not remove the saved conversion state. Cancelling a conversion or removing a document from the selection is not a request to erase all previously stored results.

Original PDFs and files you export outside the app remain in their chosen locations. Delete those separately through your file manager or storage provider. Copies already received by another app or person are outside ContextLite’s control.

The app is configured to exclude its private data from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Exported files may be backed up or synced by the storage provider you choose.

4. Copying, saving, and sharing

Copy Markdown places the generated text on the Android clipboard. Clipboard access and retention depend on your Android version, device settings, and apps with permitted access.

Save .md writes a Markdown file to the location you choose in Android’s file picker. Share .md makes a Markdown attachment available to the app you select through Android’s share sheet.

If you choose a cloud document provider, cloud save location, messaging app, or AI service, that provider may download, upload, store, or otherwise process the selected content under its own privacy policy. Such transfers are initiated by your choices; ContextLite does not automatically send documents to the developer or an AI provider.

5. Permissions and security

ContextLite uses Android’s document picker for access to selected files and may request notification permission to show conversion progress and completion. Background conversion uses Android’s foreground-service facilities.

The current app does not request Android’s Internet permission and does not include advertising, analytics, or developer-operated crash-reporting services. It does not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, camera recordings, or microphone recordings.

Internal conversion data is protected by Android’s app storage isolation. Shared files use temporary read access for the receiving app. The app does not add its own password protection or encryption to exported Markdown; protect your device and choose export destinations appropriate for the document’s contents.

6. Support and internal testing

If you contact the developer, the developer receives the email address or profile details and message contents you voluntarily provide. These are used to respond to your enquiry and resolve the issue. Avoid sending private documents unless they are necessary for your request.

If you voluntarily provide your Google account email to join internal testing, it is used to manage testing access in Google Play Console and communicate about that testing. Google processes testing information under its own policies. This process is separate from document conversion inside the app.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to resolve and follow up on the enquiry. Tester contact details are retained while needed to administer your participation. You can request removal from the developer’s tester list or deletion of support correspondence using the contact below, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements. Google’s own records remain subject to Google’s policies.

The developer does not sell your documents, support messages, or tester contact details, or use them for advertising.

7. This website and external services

This policy page has no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or document uploads. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes and may process technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google Play distribution and any external services you choose are governed by their respective policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when ContextLite’s features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the app or through its release information, as appropriate.
